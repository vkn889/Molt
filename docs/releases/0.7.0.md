# Molt 0.7.0: Simpler, and a little more alive

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vkn889/Molt/main/docs/screenshots/app-icon.png" width="128" alt="Molt app icon"></p>

Molt now lives in your MacBook's notch like it belongs there. Every page is the same size as Home, Molt has a new look, and your music, playlists and Claude Code / Codex chats are one hover away.

![Molt Home](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vkn889/Molt/main/docs/screenshots/notch-home.png)

## ✨ Highlights

### Every page, one size
Home, Hub and Play sit left of the notch. Claude Code & Codex, Chat and Settings are icons on the right. No menus to dig through.

### Meet the new Molt
Molt has been redrawn as a simple blocky pixel character: green, with a leaf sprout. It walks along the bottom of Home and curls up to sleep at night or when it's tired. **The more you care for Molt, the more it runs around.** Molt is also the new app icon and menu bar icon.

### Play
A little tamagotchi. Drag a meal, snack, water, ball, nap, brush, pat or story onto Molt, or just click one. Watch its Food, Energy and Love bars, and see it react.

![Play](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vkn889/Molt/main/docs/screenshots/notch-play.png)

### Your playlists, without opening the app
Click the album art on Home to browse your playlists as covers. Pick one and it plays in the background; Molt starts the music app hidden, so you never have to switch to it.
- **Apple Music:** connect to see the playlists in your library.
- **Spotify:** connect your account (one-time setup below). Molt also shows and controls Spotify playing on your phone or other devices.

### Claude Code & Codex
See every conversation from both tools, newest first, with today's and this week's token usage. Click one to read it. Molt can keep a private copy of every chat so they're never lost when the tools clean up old sessions. Nothing leaves your Mac.

### A cleaner Hub
Four tiles: focus timer, today's tasks, weather, and your **top 3 apps today**, shown with their real icons.

![Hub](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vkn889/Molt/main/docs/screenshots/notch-hub.png)

## 🎧 Connecting Spotify (one time)
Spotify only lets registered apps sign in:
1. Create a free app at https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard
2. Add `http://127.0.0.1:43821/callback` as its redirect URI
3. Paste its Client ID into Molt (Home, then the playlist button, then Spotify) and click **Connect**

You sign in on Spotify's own page, and Molt keeps the sign-in in your macOS keychain. Controlling other devices requires Spotify Premium.

## 🧹 Removed
To keep Molt simple, these are gone: Today, Library, Quick capture, Activity, the care dashboard, wardrobe and home decoration, web search, task mode, the old mini-games, the pixel art and melody maker, Scenes, wallpaper and Dock controls, playlist links, cost estimates, morning postcards, the floating desktop pet, and species import/export. Your existing data files are left untouched.

## 🔒 Privacy
Calendar, app usage, Mac health, and Claude Code & Codex reading are each off until you turn them on. Chat copies stay in `~/Library/Application Support/Molt/Sessions`. Weather sends only your city search to Open-Meteo.

## 📦 Install
Download **Molt-0.7.0.dmg**, drag Molt to Applications, and open it. Then hover over your notch or press **⌘⇧↩**.

> Molt is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. On first launch, right-click Molt and choose **Open**. If an older Molt is running, quit it first.

Requires macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

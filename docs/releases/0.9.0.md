# Molt 0.9.0: Your agents, in your notch

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vkn889/Molt/main/docs/screenshots/app-icon.png" width="128" alt="Molt app icon"></p>

Molt now keeps an eye on your coding agents. Answer Claude Code from the notch, see when Codex finishes, watch your plan limits and your GitHub streak, chat with any model, and dress Molt up. Molt itself got a lot livelier, too.

> 🎬 Watch the 45-second tour: **Molt-0.9.0.mp4** is attached to this release.

## ✅ Answer your agents from the notch

![Approval in the notch](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vkn889/Molt/main/docs/screenshots/notch-approval.png)

When Claude Code wants to run a command or edit a file, Molt drops down from the notch with exactly what it wants to do, how risky it looks, and why. **Allow**, allow for **this session**, **Deny**, or hand it back to the **Terminal**. Or keep typing and press **⌃⌥Y** or **⌃⌥N**.

- **Molt learns your habits.** Allow `npm test` three times and Molt offers to always allow it. Risky commands (deleting recursively, force-pushing, `sudo`, piping a download into a shell) always ask.
- **Already in your terminal?** Then it asks you there, like before.
- **Molt isn't running?** Your agents ask in the terminal as usual. Nothing breaks.

## 👾 Every agent at a glance

![Agents](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vkn889/Molt/main/docs/screenshots/notch-agents.png)

- With the notch closed, **Molt sits at its laptop**, turning the color of the agent that's working, and pops up a **!** when one needs you.
- **Finished** notes for long tasks: "Codex finished · website · Pushed to main". They wait while you're focusing.
- **Live** shows every session with its project and timer, and the agents running on your Mac (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor Agent, OpenCode, Amp, Copilot CLI, Aider and Goose) with their memory and CPU, and a **Stop** button.
- **Files changed:** in any Claude Code or Codex chat, tap **±** to see the files the agent touched, with what's still uncommitted, and the diff.
- Connect once from **Agents › Setup**. Molt keeps a backup of your Claude Code settings and keeps any Codex notify program you already had.

## 📊 Your plan, with a forecast

![Plan usage](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vkn889/Molt/main/docs/screenshots/notch-usage.png)

Your Claude and Codex 5-hour and weekly limits, with a mark for where an even pace would be and a forecast: **"At this pace, full at 3:40 PM."** When you're close to a limit, Molt gets sleepy.

## 🐙 GitHub: PRs, checks and your streak

![GitHub](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vkn889/Molt/main/docs/screenshots/notch-github.png)

Your contribution streak and grid, your open pull requests with their checks, reviews waiting on you (orange after a day), and the default branch of the repo you last worked in. If it turns red, Molt worries and lets you know. Signs in with the GitHub CLI, or a token you paste.

## 💬 Chat with any model

![Chat](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vkn889/Molt/main/docs/screenshots/notch-chat.png)

Pick **Ollama** or **LM Studio** to stay on your Mac, or **Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or OpenRouter** with your own key (kept in the keychain). Lens can use its own model, and its new **Send to agent** turns an error on your screen into a prompt for Claude Code or Codex.

## 🎩 A wardrobe for Molt

![Wardrobe](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vkn889/Molt/main/docs/screenshots/notch-wardrobe.png)

Sixteen pixel items, from a scarf to headphones, a hard hat and a halo. Earn them by caring for Molt, hanging out with friends, finishing agent tasks and keeping your GitHub streak. **This month only: the pumpkin.** Lend your outfit along with your setup.

## ✨ A livelier Molt

Molt still looks like Molt, but now it breathes, squashes when it lands, follows your pointer with its eyes, blinks at its own pace and blushes when you pat it. It throws sparkles when it's happy, confetti when your agent finishes, hearts when you care for it, and drifting z's when it sleeps. Click it on Home to say hi.

## 🔒 Privacy

- Agents talk to Molt only on your Mac (`127.0.0.1`), with a random token.
- Claude limits are off until you turn them on. Molt then reads Claude Code's sign-in from your keychain (macOS asks first), sends it only to Anthropic, and never stores it.
- GitHub talks only to `api.github.com`.
- Chat with Ollama or LM Studio stays on your Mac. Cloud services get your messages only when you choose them.

## 📦 Install

Download **Molt-0.9.0.dmg**, drag Molt to Applications, and open it. Then open **Agents › Setup** and connect Claude Code and Codex.

> Molt is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. On first launch, right-click Molt and choose **Open**. Quit any older Molt first.

Requires macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

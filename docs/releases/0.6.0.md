# Molt 0.6.0: Part of the notch

Molt now looks and behaves like an extension of the MacBook notch.

## A cleaner, native look

- Pure black panel with outward flared top corners and continuous rounded lower corners, so it reads as part of the hardware.
- The header sits in the menu-bar band on either side of the notch: Home and Hub tabs on the left, Chat, Search, Capture and the menu on the right.
- San Francisco typography, quiet grouped surfaces and native-feeling buttons replace the retro pixel styling. The bundled Shrikhand font is removed.
- The accent color defaults to system blue. Light and custom panel themes are removed; the notch is always black.

## Home

- **Now playing:** artwork with the source app's icon, title, album and artist, a live progress bar you can drag to seek, and previous, play/pause and next controls.
- **Week:** the month and a five-day strip with today highlighted, dots for days with events, then today's events or priorities, or "Nothing for today".
- **Mac health:** battery, CPU, memory and storage, plus your companion's care level.

## Music connects on its own

- Follows Spotify and Apple Music automatically, with no service picker. The player that is currently playing wins.
- Uses each player's own change notifications, so track changes appear instantly. Molt never launches a player by itself.
- macOS asks once for Automation permission. If it is declined, Home shows a shortcut to the right Settings pane.
- While music plays and Molt is closed, artwork and a level meter sit on either side of the notch. Click to open.

## Opening and closing

- Hover over the notch to open Home; move away to close it. Clicking inside keeps it open until you click elsewhere. Turn this off from the menu.
- Command-Shift-Enter and the menu bar still work.

## Hub

The previous hub cards (Today, weather, My Mac, Scenes, Usage) live under the Hub tab. The music card is now **Playlists & volume**.

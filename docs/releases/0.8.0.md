# Molt 0.8.0: Your Hub, your screen, your friends

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vkn889/Molt/main/docs/screenshots/app-icon.png" width="128" alt="Molt app icon"></p>

This is Molt's biggest update yet: a Hub you build yourself, a Lens for anything on your screen, a look inside what's slowing your Mac down, and **Molting**, where your Molt can meet your friends'.

## 🧩 Build your own Hub

Tap the pencil, or hold any widget, and the Hub jiggles just like an iPhone Home Screen. Drag to reorder, tap **–** to remove, switch between small and wide, and tap **+** to add from 17 widgets.

![Hub](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vkn889/Molt/main/docs/screenshots/notch-hub.png)

- **Shortcuts:** pick up to 8 one-click actions: record your screen, screenshot, ask about your screen, pick a color, keep awake, dark mode, mute, sleep screen, Downloads, empty Trash, focus, any app, any Apple Shortcut, or any link.
- **Screen recorder:** one click to start. A red dot and timer sit beside the notch while recording; click them to stop. Saves to Movies.
- Also: Lens, Molt, Agent usage, Up next, Mac health, Now playing, Playlists, Clock, Downloads, Quick note, Web search, Focus, Tasks, Weather and Top apps.

## 🔍 Lens: ask about anything on your screen

Press **⌘⇧L** and drag over text, an image, an error or a QR code. Molt reads it on your Mac with Apple's built-in Vision and answers with your Ollama model. No vision model to download, nothing uploaded.

## 🩺 See what's using your Mac

Click the health column on Home to see the apps using the most CPU and memory, battery health and cycles, and what's filling your storage. Molt suggests one safe fix at a time, like quitting a memory-hungry background app or emptying a big Trash, always after you confirm.

## 👋 Molting

![Molting](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vkn889/Molt/main/docs/screenshots/notch-molting.png)

- **Your account:** create it once with email, password and a @username; Molt keeps you signed in. Optional two-factor authentication, with a weekly nudge until it's on.
- **Friends:** add people by @username or from **Online now**.
- **Hangouts:** invite a friend and both Molts meet in a little playground. Walk around, wave, send hearts, dance. Time together earns you both points and raises your social status.
- **Lend your setup:** share your widgets and colors with a friend for a day, a week, or until they give it back.

## 🔒 Privacy

Molt now has an account server. It stores your email, @username, Molt's look, points and friends. Hangouts send only positions and emotes, live, to your friend. Lens, recordings, health details and your chats stay on your Mac.

## 📦 Install

Download **Molt-0.8.0.dmg**, drag Molt to Applications, and open it. Create your account, then hover over your notch.

> Molt is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. On first launch, right-click Molt and choose **Open**. Quit any older Molt first. Lens and screen recording ask for Screen Recording permission the first time.

Requires macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

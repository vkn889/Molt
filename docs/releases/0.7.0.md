# Molt 0.7.0: Simpler, and a little more alive

Every page is now the same size as Home, and anything that didn't earn its place is gone.

## Pages

- **Home, Hub and Play** sit left of the notch. **Claude Code & Codex, Chat and Settings** are icons on the right.
- **Hub** is four tiles: focus timer, today's tasks, weather, and your three most-used apps today with their icons.
- **Settings** fits on one page: Molt's name and color, accent, hover, display, launch at login, and every connection.

## Molt

- Redrawn as a simple blocky pixel character, green with a leaf sprout, in code instead of a sprite sheet.
- Molt walks along the bottom of Home, or sleeps. Well-cared-for, recently played-with Molt runs further and faster.
- **Play** is a small tamagotchi: drag a meal, snack, water, ball, nap, brush, pat or story onto Molt, or click it.

## Music accounts

- Browse your playlists from Home as a row of covers and play them without opening the music app. Molt starts it hidden in the background.
- **Apple Music:** connect to read the playlists in your library.
- **Spotify:** connect your account (one-time Client ID setup). Molt also follows and controls Spotify playing on other devices.

## Claude Code & Codex

- Lists your conversations from both tools with today's and this week's token usage, and shows each one.
- Optionally keeps a private copy of every chat in Molt's folder so they survive the tools' own cleanup.
- Codex's internal approval-review runs and injected context are hidden.

## Removed

Today, Library, Quick capture, Activity, the care dashboard, wardrobe and home decoration, web search (Molting), task mode and action history, the old mini-games, the pixel art and melody maker, Scenes, wallpaper and Dock controls, playlist links, cost estimates, morning postcards, the floating desktop pet, species import and export, and the old sprite sheet. Existing data files are left in place.
